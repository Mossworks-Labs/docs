# Selling your work

A start-to-finish walkthrough of the **seller** journey on the [Marketplace](/guide/marketplace): from listing yourself for hire to delivering your first gig and growing your tier. If you're the one doing the hiring, see [Hiring a creator](/guide/marketplace-buyer) instead.

::: tip Sellers pay $0 in platform fees
Listings, demos, messaging, contracts, payouts, and 1099 issuance are all free. The buyer pays the flat 15% fee; nothing is deducted from your quoted price. The only line item you'll ever see is storage ($4/mo for 25 GB at the current tier).
:::

## 1. Become a seller — finish onboarding

<FlatImage name="marketplace-onboarding-start" alt="Onboarding checklist — 4 of 21 items, identity / profile / stripe sections" />

Open **[Seller onboarding](/guide/marketplace-onboarding)** (`/me/start`). It's a **21-item checklist** across three sections — **Identity**, **Profile**, **Stripe**. Work top to bottom; the left rail lets you jump to any item and strikes through what's done. Your public listing turns on automatically once the whole checklist passes.

Fill out the **Profile** items thoroughly — avatar, bio, demos (`/me/demos`), packages (`/me/packages`), availability (`/me/availability`). These populate your public [Artist profile](/guide/marketplace-artist-profile), which is what buyers judge you on.

## 2. Connect Stripe for payouts

<FlatImage name="marketplace-stripe-handoff" alt="Stripe handoff — four checks waiting on Stripe" />

The **Stripe** section (`/me/stripe`) hands you off to Stripe's hosted onboarding for ID verification, tax forms (W-8BEN / W-9), and bank-account setup — we never see any of it, only the status. Four checks flip green as Stripe completes them: **account created → details submitted → charges enabled → payouts enabled**. First-timers usually finish in about 8 minutes.

::: tip Beta testing
On Stripe sandbox, use the test bank accounts and test KYC values from the [Beta testing reference](/guide/beta-testing) — real bank details are rejected until the live-mode cutover.
:::

You can't be paid until **payouts enabled** is green, so clear this before chasing gigs.

## 3. Preview your public profile

Before going further, open **Preview** (`/me/preview`) to see exactly what buyers see — demo reel, packages, reviews, and the hire sidebar. Tune your packages and demos until the page sells the work for you.

## 4. Win work

<FlatImage name="marketplace-gigs" alt="Open gigs — filter rail + gig cards" />

Two ways work comes in:

- **Apply to gigs.** Open **[Gigs](/guide/marketplace-gigs)** (`/gigs`), filter by role/budget/deadline, open a brief, and submit an application — a short pitch plus your quote. Track everything under **My applications** (`/me/applications`), and set **gig alerts** (`/me/gig-alerts`) to get pinged when matching gigs post.

<FlatImage name="marketplace-gig-alerts" alt="Gig alerts — saved searches that notify you when matching gigs post" />
- **Get requested.** Buyers who find you on [Browse](/guide/marketplace-browse) can invite you to a gig directly. Invitations land at `/me/invitations`.

Fast, specific replies matter: reply speed is one of the four [contribution weights](/guide/marketplace-gamification), and a `Fast replier` badge (avg reply ≤ 3h) helps you stand out.

## 5. Deliver under contract

When a buyer accepts your application, a contract is created against your quote and the buyer funds **escrow** — so the money is committed before you start. Manage active work from the **Active gigs** tab in [My Studio](/guide/marketplace-my-studio) or from **Contracts** (`/contracts`).

<FlatImage name="marketplace-studio" alt="My Studio — seller dashboard with tier, badges, contribution weights" />

- **Single-payment** contracts release the full amount when the buyer accepts your delivery.
- **Milestone** contracts release each milestone as the buyer accepts it — deliver and submit them one at a time from the Milestones panel.

Deliver on time and within the agreed revision count. If something goes wrong, refunds, cancellations, and [disputes](/guide/marketplace-hire) (`/me/disputes`) are handled from the contract.

## 6. Grow your tier

Every completed, well-rated, on-time gig moves your stats. Recognition is **merit-only** — see [Gamification](/guide/marketplace-gamification) for the exact thresholds:

- **Tiers** (Bronze → Platinum) gate on rating, completed jobs, and on-time % together.
- **Spotlight eligibility** (≥ 4.8★, ≥ 25 jobs) puts you on the [Browse](/guide/marketplace-browse) spotlight rail.
- **Badges, achievements, and streaks** accumulate on your profile.

My Studio always shows your **next-tier hint** — the concrete list of what's still needed — so you know exactly what moves the needle.
