# My Studio

If you're a creator, **My Studio** is your home in the Marketplace — the fourth top-nav tab, at `/me/studio`. It's where you track your standing, your active work, and jump to every per-role surface.

<FlatImage name="marketplace-studio" alt="My Studio — seller dashboard with tier, badges, contribution weights" />

## Three layers

**Hero row**
- A **progression tile** on the left: your tier badge, tier-score progress bar, top earned badges, active streak, recent achievements, and onboarding completeness.
- A **spotlight card** on the right: this-quarter job progress against the goal, per-role eligibility breakdown, and the contribution weights — **rating 40% · on-time 30% · volume 20% · reply 10%**.

**Five-stat grid** — 90-day rating, active gigs, completed gigs, this-quarter progress, and setup completion (X/21, from [onboarding](/guide/marketplace-onboarding)).

**Three internal tabs**
- **Metrics** (default) — earned and locked badges with a show/hide toggle, plus your most recent reviews.
- **Active gigs** — a table of in-progress contracts with status, deadline, and amount. Click a row for the full [contract view](/guide/marketplace-hire).

<FlatImage name="marketplace-studio-active" alt="My Studio — Active gigs tab showing in-progress contracts" />
- **Partnerships** — a placeholder for "Brands I worked with" with a per-partner "Leave review" button. Turns on when [brand partners](/guide/marketplace-brand-partners) launches.
