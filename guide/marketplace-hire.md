# Hire & contract

This page explains how an engagement becomes a contract and how money moves. For the step-by-step buyer journey, see **[Hiring a creator](/guide/marketplace-buyer)**.

## How an engagement starts today

There are two ways a contract comes into being, and **both run through a [gig](/guide/marketplace-gigs)**:

1. **A creator applies to your gig.** You post a gig, creators apply with a pitch and a quote, and you accept one.
2. **You invite a specific creator.** From an [artist profile](/guide/marketplace-artist-profile), **Request this creator** walks a preview of the upcoming direct-hire flow and then routes you to post a gig with that creator invited.

::: info The direct-hire stepper is a preview
The marketplace shows a **Brief → Schedule → Pay** stepper when you request a creator. Today this is a **non-functional preview** of the upcoming one-click direct-hire flow — it never creates a contract on its own. The live path that actually creates a contract is accepting a gig application (option 1 or 2 above). When direct hire ships, this page will be updated.
:::

## The contract

Accepting an applicant creates a **CRAFT-standard contract** with fields for the parties, deliverable, format, revisions, delivery date, usage rights, and a credit line. You can open any contract from **Contracts** (`/contracts`) or the **Active gigs** tab in [My Studio](/guide/marketplace-my-studio).

<FlatImage name="marketplace-contract-detail" alt="Contract detail — parties, escrow balance, money breakdown, and timeline" />

The payment summary breaks out:

- `{words} × {rate}` → **creator receives**
- **Marketplace fee (15%)** — the buyer's only added cost
- **You pay** — the total

## Escrow

Funds are held in **escrow** until you accept the deliverable — the creator is never paid for work you haven't approved, and your money isn't released without your action.

- **Single-payment contracts** hold one charge for the whole agreed price.
- **Milestone contracts** hold each milestone independently and release them as you accept each one. Manage these from the **Milestones** panel on the contract.

<FlatImage name="marketplace-milestones" alt="Milestone contract — per-milestone breakdown with escrow and activity" />

## After the work is delivered

From the contract view you can:

- **Accept** the deliverable → releases the escrowed funds to the creator.
- Request **revisions** within the agreed revision count.
- File for a **refund** or **cancellation**.
- Open a **dispute** (`/me/disputes`) if you and the creator can't agree — disputes go to platform mediation.
