# Buyer & Seller

Every account on Mossworks has two sides — **Buyer** and **Seller** — and you can switch between them whenever you like. Most people start as a Buyer, build a channel, and add Seller later.

## Buyer

The default. As a Buyer you can:

- Create channels, write scripts, produce audio, render episodes, and publish.
- Browse the Marketplace and **hire** sellers (voice actors, editors, animators, musicians).
- **Post gigs** so sellers can apply with a quote.

## Seller

Sellers offer their craft on the Marketplace. As a Seller you can:

- Build a profile, list demos and packages, set availability and rates.
- Apply to posted gigs or get hired directly from your profile.
- Get paid through Stripe — sellers pay zero platform fees.

See the [Marketplace guide](/guide/marketplace) for the full Seller flow, including the onboarding checklist.

## Switching personas

Open the **Profile** menu at the bottom of the sidebar and toggle between **Buyer** and **Seller**. The sidebar reshapes to surface the tools that matter for the side you're on; your data and signed-in identity stay the same.

## Sign out

Open the **Profile** menu and click **Sign out** at the bottom. This ends your session and returns you to the login screen.
