# Browse

**Browse** is the buyer-facing discovery surface of the [Marketplace](/guide/marketplace) — where channels search for creators to hire. It's the first of the four top-nav tabs (**Browse · Gigs · Brand partners · My Studio**) and lives at `/sellers`.

<FlatImage name="marketplace-browse" alt="Marketplace browse — filter rail, spotlight rail, creator grid" />

## Layout

- **Filter rail** on the left — category, style/vibe, language, turnaround, and price.
- **Hero stats** across the top.
- **Spotlight rail** of top creators — anyone at **≥ 4.8★ with ≥ 25 completed jobs** in any role.
- **Creator grid** for everyone else.

Each card shows role, rating, jobs completed, and rate. For voice actors, the card also previews a mini-waveform demo; for video roles, a filmstrip preview.

## Categories

- **Voice actors** — narration, spokesperson, ad reads
- **Spokespersons** — on-camera hosts
- **Musicians** — custom tracks, ambient, orchestral, electronic
- **Editors** — pacing surgery, shorts-first cuts, long-form docs
- **Animators** — 2D motion, 3D, explainer, data-viz

## What's merit-only

Ranking and the Spotlight rail are computed from public stats — rating, completed-job count, on-time percentage, reply speed. There are **no paid boosts**: a creator cannot buy their way up the grid. See [Gamification](/guide/marketplace-gamification) for the exact thresholds behind Spotlight eligibility and tiers.
