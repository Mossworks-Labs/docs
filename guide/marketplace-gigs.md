# Gigs

**Gigs** is the flip side of [Browse](/guide/marketplace-browse). Instead of buyers searching for creators, creators see **channels posting what they need** and apply with a message and a quote. It's the second Marketplace top-nav tab and lives at `/gigs`.

<FlatImage name="marketplace-gigs" alt="Open gigs — filter rail + gig cards" />

Posting a gig — rather than the direct-hire stepper — is also the path the Marketplace uses today to engage a *specific* creator: you invite them to a gig you've posted. See [Hire & contract](/guide/marketplace-hire) for why.

## For creators — find and apply

1. Open **Gigs** in the marketplace top nav.
2. Filter by role, budget, deadline, or status.
3. Click a gig card to see the full brief — description, deliverables, tags, budget.
4. Submit an application: a short pitch plus your quote.

The buyer sees your application under their gig. If they shortlist or accept you, the next step is the standard [Hire & contract](/guide/marketplace-hire) flow against your quote. You can track everything you've applied to under **My applications** (`/me/applications`), and set up **gig alerts** (`/me/gig-alerts`) to be notified when matching gigs are posted.

## For buyers — post a gig

<FlatImage name="marketplace-post-gig" alt="Post a gig form with live preview" />

Click **+ Post a gig** at the top of the marketplace (`/gigs/new`). The form has:

- **Title** — the headline creators see in the list
- **Role** — voice / spokesperson / music / editor / animator
- **Description** — the brief (context, tone, samples, deadlines)
- **Deliverables** — file formats, revisions, timeline
- **Budget min / max** — displayed as a range on the card
- **Deadline** — free-text (e.g. "Apr 28" or "Ongoing")
- **Tags** — comma-separated search keywords
- **Your name / channel** — attribution shown on the gig

A live preview card sits to the right so you see the cards exactly as applicants will. **Nothing is charged at post-time** — funds only move through escrow once you *accept* a specific applicant, which is when the [contract](/guide/marketplace-hire) is created.
