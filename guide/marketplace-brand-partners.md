# Brand partners

**Brand partners** is the **brand-sponsorship** side of the [Marketplace](/guide/marketplace) — distinct from the creators-for-hire flow on [Browse](/guide/marketplace-browse) and [Gigs](/guide/marketplace-gigs). It's the third top-nav tab and lives at `/brand-partners`.

## The two populations

- **Channels seeking sponsors** — audience, niche, sample reads, CPM ranges.
- **Brands seeking channels** — products, campaigns, deliverable expectations, budgets.

Both render as partner cards with hue-themed covers, tier badges (see [Gamification](/guide/marketplace-gamification)), an active-listings count, and a spotlight flag for top performers.

## Placement-scoped reviews

Unlike creator reviews, which attach to a gig, brand-partner reviews are **placement-scoped** — each review references a specific placement, so both sides can back-reference the engagement. The star-rating mechanics and merit-only ranking are the same as creator reviews: **no paid boosts**.

## Where it shows up for sellers

When brand partners launches, the **Partnerships** tab inside [My Studio](/guide/marketplace-my-studio) — currently a placeholder for "Brands I worked with" — turns on, with a per-partner **Leave review** button.
