# Artist profile

The **artist profile** is a creator's public page — what a buyer lands on after clicking a card on [Browse](/guide/marketplace-browse). It lives at `/sellers/<slug>` (for example `/sellers/aria-sato`).

<FlatImage name="marketplace-artist-profile" alt="Artist public profile — bio, languages, packages, ratings, hire sidebar" />

## What's on the page

- **Cover image** and bio.
- **Demo reel** with inline audio playback.
- **Reviews** — star ratings and written feedback from past gigs.
- A **sticky hire sidebar** that follows you as you scroll.

## The hire sidebar

- **Headline rate**.
- **Minimum order** and **typical reply time**.
- **Package tiers** — e.g. *"Script under 500w · 24h · $60"* or *"Documentary narration · 3 days · $210"*.
- **Request this creator →** — starts the engagement (see [Hire & contract](/guide/marketplace-hire)).
- **Send a message first** — opens real messaging (`/messages`), not a mockup, so you can scope the work before committing.

## Previewing your own profile

If you're a creator, you can see exactly what buyers see before going live: open **Preview** from [My Studio](/guide/marketplace-my-studio) (`/me/preview`). Your public listing only turns on once [seller onboarding](/guide/marketplace-onboarding) is complete.
